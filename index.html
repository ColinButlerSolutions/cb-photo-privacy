Claris Bio Photo Support

Claris Bio Photo (formerly CB Photo Capture) is a private photo and video capture utility used by authorized Claris Bio study personnel for standardized image acquisition workflows.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Claris Bio Photo allows authorized users to enter a subject identifier, capture white-light and blue-light photos and videos, and export capture packages for use in authorized study workflows.

Data handled by the app

The app may handle the following information on the device:

  • subject identifiers entered by the user
  • photos captured using the device camera
  • videos captured using the device camera
  • timestamps, study and visit details, and the eye and capture mode associated with captured media
  • operator names and identifiers, site numbers, and principal investigator names
  • device installation identifiers, device and operating system information, and app and update versions
  • requested and recorded camera settings, technical capture checks, and review and audit history
  • exported ZIP packages created by the app

Data collection

Claris Bio Photo does not display advertising, use advertising identifiers, or share data with data brokers. It does not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. Technical update and diagnostic information is handled as described below.

The app does not sell personal information.

The app is designed as a local capture and export utility. Captured files are stored locally on the device unless an authorized user exports or transfers them as part of the applicable Claris Bio workflow.

Software updates and technical information

Version 2.0 uses Expo's update service to check for and download compatible software updates. These connections transmit technical information, including a randomized installation identifier, platform and software runtime information, and installed update identifiers. Expo also receives the connection's IP address. If an update-related fatal error occurs, the app may send an error message to help diagnose the failure. Expo describes its handling of operating system information, update tokens, network information, and usage and performance information in its privacy policy.

The update service delivers application software; it is not a destination for clinical capture packages. The app is not configured to upload captured photos, videos, or study export packages to Expo as part of checking for or downloading updates.

Local storage and retention

Operator attribution is stored with captures and included in exported records. The app retains original media, associated metadata, and recovery copies to preserve capture history and support recovery from interruptions. Importing older sessions preserves the original files and creates verified copies for use in the app. Exporting a package does not automatically delete the local media, metadata, or recovery copies.

Local and exported study records must be retained and removed according to applicable Claris Bio study and data protection procedures. Removing one exported copy does not remove other copies retained on the device or transferred to an authorized destination.

Clinical use

Claris Bio Photo captures, stores, supports review of, and exports photos and videos. It performs technical checks, such as verifying camera settings and identifying highlight clipping. These checks do not interpret clinical findings, diagnose disease, score clinical findings, recommend treatment, or make clinical decisions.

Data handling after export

Data exported from the app is handled according to applicable Claris Bio study procedures, privacy procedures, and data protection requirements.
